# Streamlit control room

- Mode: Operate.
- Audience: course reviewers and IoT/AI developers evaluating a safety-first natural-language control pipeline.
- Job: enter a Chinese household request, inspect how it passes through classification, planning, interlocks, confirmation, and simulated execution.
- Primary task: send a command and understand the resulting intent, device actions, risk level, and outcome within one viewport.
- Proof: a visible five-stage route, 47 allowlisted simulated devices, concrete command JSON, safety findings, persistent automations, and explicitly labeled synthetic research data.
- Constraints: no real-device claim; optional LLM may classify only; dangerous actions never execute before confirmation; desktop and mobile must remain legible.
- Direction: railway signal desk rendered as warm instrument paper, graphite route rails, and signal-state lamps.
- Memorable moment: the route lamps advance only as a request clears each interlock, with amber stopping at confirmation and green reaching execution.
- Open decision: production branding and licensing remain user-owned decisions.
